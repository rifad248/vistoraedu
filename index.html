FAQ

Questions Families Ask Us

Honest answers — no guarantees we can't back, no jargon.

How can I get B.Sc Nursing admission?

Start by confirming your eligibility (most programs require Plus Two with Physics, Chemistry and Biology), shortlisting recognised colleges, and preparing documents. Vistora guides you through each step — eligibility checks, college options in Kerala, Karnataka and Tamil Nadu, and applications.

What is the eligibility for B.Sc Nursing?

Most Indian universities require 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English, with minimum marks that vary by university and state. Exact criteria differ per college — we verify the specific requirement for every college before you apply.

Can Kerala students apply for nursing colleges in Karnataka?

Yes. Kerala students can apply to nursing colleges in Karnataka, subject to each college's eligibility criteria and documentation. We guide you through state-specific requirements, timelines and paperwork.

Can I get nursing admission in Tamil Nadu?

Yes. Kerala students can apply to nursing colleges in Tamil Nadu, subject to each college's eligibility and documentation requirements. We explain the options and guide your application step by step.

What documents are required for nursing admission?

Typically Plus Two marks card, transfer and migration certificates, ID proof and photographs. Exact lists vary by college and state — use our admission readiness check and we confirm the precise list for your chosen colleges.

What can I study after Plus Two Commerce?

B.Com, BBA, BCA and many other degree programs are open to commerce students depending on your subjects and goals. Our Course Finder and counsellors help you compare options honestly.

Is BCA a good career option?

For students interested in computing, BCA leads to careers in software development, data and IT services, and further study such as MCA. Whether it suits you depends on your interests and strengths — we help you judge that honestly.

What can I do after BBA?

BBA graduates move into management, marketing, HR and finance roles, join family businesses, or pursue an MBA and professional certifications. Your specialisation and interests shape the best direction.

How do I choose the right degree after Plus Two?

Combine your academic background, interests, career goals and practical factors like location and timing. Our free Course Finder gives structured starting points, then a counsellor helps you decide with your family.

How can I check my course eligibility?

Share your qualification, stream and marks with our team — we check eligibility against the specific requirements of each college before you apply. You can also use our Admission Readiness Check on this page.

How does admission counselling work?

It starts with a free conversation about your goals and background, followed by a personalised shortlist, honest comparison of options, and step-by-step application support. Parents are welcome in every session.

Is the first conversation really free?

Yes. Your first conversation is free — we'll understand your situation, explain how we can help, and you decide whether to continue. No pressure at any point.
